01 / JOB LIFECYCLE

Claim to completion

Creates a one-second sleep job. Watch it move from queued to running to succeeded in the inspector.

What I builtA PostgreSQL-backed queue and a worker that claims and completes jobs.
What I learnedSubmission and execution have separate lifecycles; a successful HTTP request does not mean the work is finished.
02 / FAILURE HANDLING

Retry to dead letter

Creates an intentionally failing job with two allowed attempts. Watch its next eligible time, then its final status.

What I builtScheduled retries with backoff and a dead-letter state after the attempt limit.
What I learnedTransient failures need a retry policy; permanent failures need a visible place for investigation.
03 / RECOVERY SAFETY

Stale worker rejected

The integration test expires a claim, lets another claim take over, and checks that the old generation cannot finish it.

What I builtLease generations and conditional completion and failure updates.
What I learnedA lease protects the job row; external side effects still need their own idempotency or fencing.
